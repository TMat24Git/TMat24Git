## Some information about my school

Founded in 1816, Mines Saint-Étienne is a public engineering school under the supervision of the Ministry of Economy, Finance, and Industry. As a member of the Institut Mines-Télécom (IMT), the school trains high-level generalist and specialized engineers in human-sized cohorts, offering personalized academic paths. The skills of its graduates are recognized both in France and internationally.
Mines Saint-Étienne stands out for its dynamic research activity, focused on the needs of industry and healthcare. The school hosts five training and research centers, recognized for their excellence in their respective fields.
Spread across three campuses (Saint-Étienne, Aix-Marseille-Provence, and Lyon-Charbonnières) and present in two regions (Auvergne-Rhône-Alpes and Provence-Alpes-Côte d’Azur), Mines Saint-Étienne is deeply rooted in its territories and maintains strong connections with the local ecosystem. Nearly 500 people, including faculty members, researchers, and technical and administrative staff, contribute to the operation of its various sites.
